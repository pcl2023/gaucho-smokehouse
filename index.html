Real wood. Whole cuts. No shortcuts.

Every piece of meat is trimmed, cured and seasoned by hand, then smoked for up to fifteen hours. Nothing leaves the pit before it is ready.

Two souths. One fire.

The American South taught the world patience: whole cuts over hickory and oak, hours of smoke, nothing rushed. The south of South America brought fire out onto the open plain, where the gaucho made asado a way of life.

GAUCHO Smokehouse joins those two traditions on one cutting board. Our rubs, our chimichurri and our gaucho sauce come from one side of the hemisphere; our pits and our smoke come from the other.

Different lands. Same fire.
