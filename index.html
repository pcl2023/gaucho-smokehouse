Every cut is trimmed and seasoned by hand, then smoked for hours: ten for the pastrami, fifteen for the brisket. Nothing leaves the pit before it is ready.

What our guests say

Two souths. One fire.

The American South taught the world patience: whole cuts, hours of smoke, nothing rushed. Down in South America, the gaucho made open-fire cooking a way of life.

GAUCHO Smokehouse puts both on one cutting board. Brisket, pulled pork and the pit come from one south. Picanha, chimichurri and our gaucho sauce come from the other.

Meet the pitmaster

Adrián Yánez is the chef who created GAUCHO. Every rub, every sauce and every cut on the menu starts with him.

He built the brand at markets and private events, one tray at a time, before bringing it to Nashville.
